# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PowerContext Desktop primarily serves ordinary business users who need to use persistent AI context and memory without understanding Server internals, Scope identifiers, authentication protocols, or diagnostic terminology. They need a guided path to connect, choose the correct workspace, save useful knowledge, find it again, and understand whether the product is ready for use.

## Product Purpose

PowerContext Desktop makes PowerContext's persistent memory capabilities approachable through a safe, trustworthy desktop interface. It should reduce the effort and uncertainty of initial setup, connection, context selection, memory capture, retrieval, and recovery. Success means a non-technical user can reach their first successful memory workflow without assistance while advanced diagnostics remain available without dominating the experience.

## Brand Personality

Professional, trustworthy, and restrained. The product should feel calm and capable without becoming technical, cold, or bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Avoid the appearance and interaction model of developer tools, infrastructure consoles, and dense operations dashboards. Do not expose protocol names, status codes, opaque identifiers, raw diagnostic details, or implementation terminology as the primary user experience. Avoid decorative visual effects that weaken clarity or trust.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the user's task, not the system architecture.
2. Reveal technical detail progressively and only when it helps a decision or recovery.
3. Make the current connection, workspace, and consequence of every action unambiguous.
4. Turn setup and recovery into guided, confidence-building flows.
5. Preserve safety and precision without making the interface feel defensive or intimidating.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, screen readers, sufficient contrast, dark mode, and reduced-motion preferences. Use plain Chinese and English labels, never relying on color alone to communicate state. Long addresses and identifiers must remain readable without breaking layouts, while non-technical summaries remain primary.
